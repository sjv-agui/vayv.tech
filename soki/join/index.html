// how this works
  • each round, the host opens a location and everyone is given a random character
  • your character has a backstory split into pieces — those pieces go to other players, never yourself
  • so you hold fragments of someone else's story, and they hold pieces of yours
  • during chat, ask each other questions to surface the truth
  • then the host opens a vote — answer the oracle question
  • high agreement triggers soki (a false sequence). low agreement reveals the truth
  • final score is collective — you win or lose together
