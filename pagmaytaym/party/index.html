01 — Three taps. That’s all they need.

How a guest uses it

1

Scan

Point the phone camera at the QR code. It opens like any web page — nothing to install, no account, no password.

2

Snap

Type your name once — the phone remembers it. Then take a photo and add a few words if you feel like it.

3

See it

Look up. Your photo is already on the big screen. Tap once more and send another.

02 — Plug in one laptop. Done.

The big screen
does the work

Plug a laptop into the TV or projector and open one link. Photos fade in one after another, and a brand-new one jumps the queue — so nobody waits to see theirs.

If the hall wi-fi stutters, the screen holds the picture and catches up on its own.

No app download required

03 — Your night, your way.

Everything the organiser gets

What you get

  • Your event, set up in a minute. Give it a name, pick a password, and it’s ready.
  • A QR code to print for the tables or show on the screen.
  • Three links: one for guests, one for the big screen, one just for you.
  • A dashboard on your phone. See how many photos have come in, rename the event, and pause uploads with one tap when the speeches start.
Coming in October Videos, the album to download and keep afterwards, and approval before a photo reaches the screen.

How the night feels

  • Guests take photos without being asked. The big screen does the asking.
  • Lola sees her name on the wall. She sees it while she’s still dancing.
  • No chasing people to post. No group chat scattered across three apps.
  • You stay present. The screen handles the memories.
04 — Because you deserve more than a group chat.

Why not just a group chat

  • 01

    It happens in the room.

    A group chat is something you scroll through next week. This is on the wall while Lola is still dancing.

  • 02

    Your photos stay in Europe.

    Stored on European servers from day one — not sent around the world and back.

  • 03

    A real person behind it.

    Sean can host or emcee the night himself, not just hand you a link.

  • 04

    Made for our celebrations.

    Christenings, debuts, 80ths, reunions, fiestas — built by and for the Filipino community in Oslo.
