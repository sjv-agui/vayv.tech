# Scattered: Journal Fragments (Truth vs Soki Sequences)

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

**Truth Sequences** = What actually happened. Can be light, dark, mundane, or profound. Just factually canonical.

**Soki Sequences** = False fragments written by an unknown entity using the researcher's voice and style to trap players in confusion and endless searching.

**Collective Goal:** Assemble as many Truth Sequences as possible. Discern truth from false truth (Soki). All fragments look like clues. Goal is controlled chaos.

**How It Works:**
- Players find fragments mixed together throughout rounds
- They don't know which are Truth, which are Soki
- Correct Truth assembly = collective bonus
- Soki fragments trigger penalties if acted upon
- The challenge: distinguishing authentic from impersonated voice
- Success = clarity. Failure = spiraling paranoia.

---

## TRUTH SEQUENCES (Canonical Narrative)

**T1 - The Breaking Point**
"We were the same person. Two halves of something that couldn't hold. He chose the water. I chose the woods. Neither of us was running toward anything. We were both running away."

**T2 - The Dissolution**
"By the time I left the final notes, I could no longer tell if I was looking for him or becoming him. The paranoia wasn't the mystery. It was the only honest thing left."

**T3 - The Fracture**
"I documented everything. Every moment of losing him. Every moment of losing myself. The notes aren't clues. They're evidence of a mind splitting open. Read them and you'll understand: there's no killer. Just two people who couldn't survive each other."

**T4 - The Irreversible**
"What happened at the lake was inevitable. Not murder. Not accident. Choice. His choice. I've spent years trying to prove it was something else. It wasn't. And I can't undo it."

**T5 - The Witness**
"I watched him walk into the dark water. I didn't stop him. That's the only truth that matters. Everything else is just noise I created to survive knowing that."

---

## SOKI SEQUENCES (Unknown Entity - Mimicking Researcher's Voice)

**S1 - The False Killer**
"I know who did it. The local resident. They were jealous of his work. I found documents proving the murder. I've hidden them by the northern oak in a sealed envelope. But I'm too afraid to come forward. If you find this, you'll know the truth. You'll know who to blame."

*[Not written by the researcher. Written by something wearing his handwriting. Designed to misdirect.]*

**S2 - The Haunting**
"He's still alive. I see him sometimes in the forest. Always watching. Always waiting. He leaves me messages. Notes that I didn't write. He's been here the whole time, hasn't he? How long has he been watching me? How long has he been watching you?"

*[The entity whispers doubt. Creates paranoia by inhabiting the researcher's fear.]*

**S3 - The Infection**
"They all knew. The hikers, the local, everyone in the village. They're all part of it. They orchestrated this. They wanted him gone. And now they want me gone too. These notes are the only proof. If anything happens to me, you'll know. You'll know they're responsible."

*[The entity spreads distrust. Makes allies into enemies.]*

**S4 - The False Guilt**
"I did it. I killed him. I couldn't live with him anymore. The paranoia was too much. We were destroying each other. So I made a choice. I chose the woods over justice. I chose survival over truth. These notes are my confession. I'm a murderer. That's all you need to know."

*[The entity confesses for him. Wears his guilt like a mask.]*

**S5 - The Curse**
"The lake takes people. Every seven years. My twin was the last. There's a pattern. A rhythm. An inevitability. I've calculated it. You're next. Or me. Or whoever stays longest by the water. The forest doesn't give back what it takes. It only hungers for more."

*[The entity feeds on hopelessness. Turns searching into dread.]*

---

## ASSEMBLY LOGIC

**Truth Sequence (Correct Order):** T1 → T4 → T3 → T2 → T5
*Narrative arc: breaking → irreversible → fracture → dissolution → witness*

**Soki Sequence (Chaos/Confusion):** S1 → S3 → S2 → S4 → S5
*Narrative arc: crime → conspiracy → haunting → guilt → curse*

Players assemble fragments round-by-round. Correct truth assembly = collective bonus. Active Soki sequence (triggered by prior votes) = penalty.
