04 — The Storyline

Two brothers.
One disappeared.
Nothing adds up.

A researcher spent years tracking his twin brother — through forest, water, and obsession. He documented everything. But when his notes were discovered by a local resident, something was wrong.

Some fragments read like a man falling apart. Others read like something else entirely — something wearing his voice like a mask.

Your task is not just to find the killer. Your task is to figure out what's real.

08 — FAQs

Questions.
Answered.

No experience is required. If you can read, talk, and think — you're ready. The experience is designed to work for first-timers and mystery veterans alike.
Both work. Solo participants join a mixed group — and meeting strangers is actually part of the design. You can also bring your own group of 4–8 for a private session.
Dress for Oslo weather — layers, waterproof jacket. The experience runs rain or shine. In cases of extreme weather, we'll reschedule or refund. You'll be notified in advance.
Weather-appropriate clothing, comfortable shoes, water, and a curious mind. Snacks are welcome. No phones required — though you can use one for notes if you'd like.
We recommend ages 15+ for the best experience. Younger participants are welcome with a supervising adult, but the story themes are designed for adult sensibilities.
Moderate walking outdoors across the fortress grounds. If you can walk 2–3km at a relaxed pace, you'll be comfortable. There are no climbing or athletic requirements.
Free cancellation with 48 hours notice. Cancellations made within 48 hours of the event are non-refundable, but can be transferred to another date or person.
