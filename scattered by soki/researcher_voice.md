# Scattered: The Researcher's Voice

## Voice DNA
Combination of **Holden Caulfield** (fractured, digressive, contradictory) + **Kurtz** (cryptic, poetic, increasingly incoherent).

---

## Core Characteristics

**Emotional Volatility**
- Cycles between raw vulnerability and detached observation
- Shifts unexpectedly from confession to deflection
- Voice breaks when touching on the twin

**Digressive & Circling**
- Returns obsessively to the same moment, reframing it each time
- Parenthetical asides that derail the thought
- Thinks in tangents. Can't maintain linear narrative.

**Poetic but Fragmented**
- Sudden lyrical passages interrupt mundane observations
- Uses metaphor to avoid direct statement
- Beauty emerging from breakdown

**Unreliable Narrator**
- Argues with himself mid-sentence
- Contradicts previous notes without acknowledgment
- Can't trust his own memory or perception

**Increasing Incoherence**
- Later notes become shorter, more cryptic
- Sentence structure deteriorates
- Gaps between entries grow
- Handwriting descriptions suggest deterioration

---

## Voice Markers (Use Consistently)

- **Parenthetical interruptions** — (but that's not true, is it?) / (god, I sound insane)
- **Self-correction** — No, that's not what I meant. Actually...
- **Repetition with variation** — Same moment told three different ways
- **Sudden shifts in register** — Clinical detail interrupted by emotional crack
- **Fragmented syntax** — Incomplete thoughts. Run-ons. Then stops.
- **Addressing unseen reader** — You'd have to understand. But you won't.
- **Poetic metaphors** — The lake isn't water. It's time. Or forgetting.

---

## Tone Spectrum

- **Clarity** (early notes): Observations feel almost journalistic
- **Doubt** (middle notes): Questions himself constantly
- **Descent** (later notes): Poetic fragmentation increases
- **Dissolution** (final notes): Almost incomprehensible. Pure image. No logic.

---

## Writing Rules for This Voice

1. No neat conclusions. Thoughts break mid-realization.
2. Contradiction is honest. Don't resolve it.
3. Oscillate between "I" and "he" — sometimes unclear who's speaking.
4. Use white space. Gaps between thoughts matter.
5. Poetic imagery when logic fails.
6. Addressing an invisible audience (confession without listener).
7. Later notes get progressively shorter and more cryptic.

---

## What This Voice Prevents

- Clarity (intentional)
- Linear narrative (intentional)
- Easy interpretation (intentional)
- Player certainty (intentional)

**Goal:** Players read the same passage three times and find different meanings.
