# Scattered: Final Narration (Psychological Thriller)

The researcher wasn't running from his twin. He was running toward him—into the dark water, into the woods, into the part of himself that needed to disappear.

You solved the mystery. But solving isn't understanding. The notes were breadcrumbs, yes. But breadcrumbs left by a man already lost, already choosing which version of himself to leave behind.

The killer you identified tonight isn't a villain. They're a mirror. Every choice you made—what you believed, what you feared, what you couldn't name—shaped who you accused. The forest didn't hide the truth. It reflected it back.

What remains isn't answers. It's the strangers beside you. The ones who saw what you saw and chose differently. That's the real mystery. Not what happened to him. But what happens when you stop looking for solutions and start looking at each other.

The woods are still watching. But now, so are you.
