# Scattered: Event Description

A researcher vanished chasing his twin's shadow. His notes were discovered by a local—fragmented, cryptic, incomplete. You arrive with strangers drawn by something you can't quite name. Together, you'll decode clues, make choices that matter, and uncover what happened before time runs out.

## The Experience

90 minutes of collaborative mystery-solving. You'll work alongside people you've just met. Their choices will shape yours. Your choices will shape theirs.

## Why Come

- Curiosity rewarded. Mystery that actually surprises you.
- Safety built in. You're solving *with* people, not against them.
- Real connection. Strangers become a room.
- Bring friends or come alone. Both work.

## Tickets

Your ticket directly supports the creator of this experience and helps fund future mysteries. Thank you for joining the beta version of this social experience.

## Bring

Weather-appropriate clothing, water, snacks, a curious mind.

## For Whom

Story lovers. Outdoor people (moderate walking). Anyone ready to play mysteries with strangers.

No experience necessary. Just show up.
